## Phase Goal

On an Xcelsior contact record, staff open one "Actions" menu in the name row and fire any of four actions (Send Invite, Send individual consent, Resend Consent Hub link, Copy contact ID) by mouse or keyboard, each with its own visible result and cooldown, while every other location and every plain-button config behaves exactly as in v0.1.2.

Plans 04-01 and 04-02 built and proved the configuration-driven menu. This plan ships it: the Xcelsior menu in the public sample config (fixing the `scope: "all"` Send Invite bug), headless proof of every item against that exact file, the offline harness, and the README.
