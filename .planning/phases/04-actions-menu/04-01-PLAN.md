## Phase Goal

On an Xcelsior contact record, staff open one "Actions" menu in the name row and fire any of four actions by mouse or keyboard, each with its own visible result and cooldown, while every other location and every plain-button config behaves exactly as in v0.1.2.

This plan builds the configuration-driven menu widget itself (any `menus[]` entry, proven with an inline test config). Plan 04-02 adds the per-item rules (cooldown proof, "Not configured", the synthetic-email pre-check, overrides, scope). Plan 04-03 ships the Xcelsior menu in `config/agency-config.json`, the harness, and the README.

<objective>
Deliver the Actions menu widget end to end. Task 1 (tracer) wires one path through every layer: a `menus[]` entry in config, validation, `resolveMenus`, rendering a trigger and a panel inside the existing contact `.ghlc-group`, opening on click, activating one webhook item through the unchanged v0.1.2 pipeline, closing, and the trigger flash, proven by a headless scenario. Task 2 adds the WAI-ARIA menu-button keyboard model (D-05) and the shim support to test it honestly (native button activation from Enter/Space). Task 3 adds every remaining close path and the single document listener lifecycle (D-06), proves no re-render loop and an unchanged observer count (D-08, MENU-07), checks the panel positioning statically (D-07), and pins D-03 with a byte-for-byte DOM snapshot taken from the `v0.1.2` tag.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria 1 (menu renders; plain configs unchanged), 2 (keyboard, close paths, not clipped), and the menu half of 3 and 5 become true and automated.
Output: updated `src/ghl-customizer.js` (constants, config, context, buttons, boot), `src/ghl-customizer.css`, `test/dom-shim.mjs`, `test/run.mjs`.
</objective>
