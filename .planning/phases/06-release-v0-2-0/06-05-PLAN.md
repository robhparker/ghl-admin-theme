## Phase Goal

v0.2.0 ships to the agency with confidence: the release is versioned, every menu item and refusal path has been proven on a real Xcelsior owner and member, and staff can be put back on v0.1.2 by changing one tag.

This plan is the one-way door. Rob decides (D-02), and only then is the release tagged and published and jsDelivr verified byte for byte. Two command gates bound his choices. The publication gate (`prepush-scan.cjs --history`) must pass, or nothing is pushed. The checklist gate decides whether "approve" is on offer and, later, whether staff may move and the phase may count as verified (D-03). The rollback target stays reachable. The snippet switch and the rollback rehearsal are Rob's, recorded in the checklist.

<objective>
Present Rob with everything the push will publish and both gates' results, and ask for approval at a blocking-human checkpoint. On approval, with the gates still passing: create the annotated tag `v0.2.0` on the commit shown, push main with tags (fast-forward, nothing else), wait for jsDelivr, and prove that the served assets are byte-identical to the tag and that @v0.1.2 still serves.

Purpose: success criteria 3 and 4 (REL-02 tag after approval; REL-03 rollback target live and rehearsed). No release path publishes a patient id.
Output: tag `v0.2.0` on origin, jsDelivr verified, a SUMMARY with the sha256 table. No file in the working tree changes.
</objective>
